**Lamps collects no data.**

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app never connects to the internet. Your lamp names and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad.

## Data we collect

Lamps does not collect any data about you, send it to the developer, or share it with anyone. The app has no accounts, sign-up, analytics, ads or tracking. It makes no network requests, so no data ever leaves your device through the app.

## Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your device:

- Lamp names and settings (for example protocol variant, device ID, brightness and warmth): in a JSON file in the app's Application Support folder.
- Your language preference: in iOS UserDefaults.

The app does not send this information anywhere, and the developer cannot access it. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include these files in your backup. That backup is made by Apple according to your settings; the developer has no access to it.

## Bluetooth

Lamps uses the Bluetooth permission only to broadcast commands to your lamps. Commands (for example on, off, brightness, warmth) are sent from your device as short Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements, which nearby lamps listen for.

These broadcasts contain no personal information. They carry only the lamp command and the data the protocol needs: a device ID the app picks at random for each lamp, the lamp's slot number, a message counter, a random number and the check values the lamp expects. The app does not scan for, connect to, or collect information about other Bluetooth devices around you. Your location is not used.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. When you download the app from the App Store, Apple's own privacy policy applies.

If you turned on sharing analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage statistics with the developer. Apple does this, not the app, with your permission and under Apple's privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a lamp inside the app. To delete all data, delete the app from your device. Lamp names, settings and the language preference are removed with it.

## Children

Lamps does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app's use of data ever changes, this policy will be updated before that change ships.
