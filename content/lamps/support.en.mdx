## What is Lamps?

Lamps is an independent app that lets you control Bluetooth ceiling lamps from your iPhone or iPad, without a hub.

These lamps only listen to Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements. The app broadcasts command packets directly from your device. No hub or extra hardware, and it works offline.

## Features

- Add a lamp with a guided pairing wizard
- Multiple lamps
- Power on and off, brightness ring dial
- Warmth slider (cold and warm white)
- Presets: Daylight, Reading, Cozy, Evening, Night
- Identify: blinks the lamp so you can tell which one it is
- Pair again and unpair
- Help for lamps that respond together
- Turkish and English, with an in-app language switch
- Free. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

## Adding and pairing a lamp

Before you start, turn on Bluetooth on your iPhone or iPad and allow the app to use Bluetooth. You need to be able to reach the lamp's wall switch.

1. In the app, tap **Add lamp** and give the lamp a name you'll recognize.
2. Only the lamp you are pairing should have power. Turn off other lamps at the wall switch, or they'll pair too.
3. Turn the lamp off at the wall switch and keep it off for 5 seconds.
4. Turn the lamp on at the wall switch, then tap **Pair** within 5 seconds. The app sends the pairing command in 4 protocol variants, one after another.
5. If the lamp blinked, tap **Yes**.
6. In step two, tap **Test** and watch the lamp. If it turned on or off, tap **Yes**. If it didn't react, tap **No, try the next one**. The app saves the variant your lamp reacts to.

## Troubleshooting

### The lamp doesn't react

- **Try another variant.** On the lamp's page, tap **Edit**, change **Protocol variant**, tap **Save** and try **Power**. To test the variants from the start, delete the lamp and add it again with **Add lamp**.
- **Power-cycle and start over.** Keep the lamp off at the wall switch for 5 seconds, turn it on, and pair within 5 seconds. The lamp only accepts the pairing command right after it is powered on.
- **Move closer.** Bluetooth advertisements have a limited range. Stand in the same room, close to the lamp.
- **Check Bluetooth.** Bluetooth must be on, and Lamps must be allowed under **Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth**.

If the app says **No variant worked**, cut the lamp's power and tap **Start over**, or tap **Save anyway** and pick the variant later in **Edit**.

### Lamps respond together

If two lamps respond to one key, or the wrong lamp turns on, they share the same ID. Pair them again one at a time. The same steps are in the app under **Fix mixed-up lamps** on the lamp's page.

1. Turn off all lamps at the wall switch.
2. In the app, open the first lamp, tap **Edit**, set **Device ID** to **Random** and tap **Save**.
3. Turn only that lamp back on at the wall.
4. Within 5 seconds, tap **Pair again** and wait for it to blink.
5. Test **Power**. Only that lamp should respond.
6. Repeat for each lamp, one at a time. Then turn them all back on.

Tip: before you start, give each lamp a name you'll recognize.

### Which lamp is which?

On the lamp's page, tap **Identify**. That lamp blinks.

### Unpairing

When you tap **Unpair**, the lamp forgets this device and won't respond until it's paired again. If it still responds, turn it off and on at the wall, then tap **Unpair** within 5 seconds.

### Changing the language

Tap the globe icon at the top of the lamp list, then under **Language** choose Türkçe, English or **System**.
